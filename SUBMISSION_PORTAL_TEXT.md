## Project title
Heat-Safe Shift Planner: next-day work-rest schedules for Tamil Nadu construction workers

## Theme
AI, GIS & Emerging Technologies (alternative: Environment & Sustainability)

## Theme description & UNSDG alignment
Decision-support system using geospatial weather data and a physical heat-stress model (WBGT, ISO 7243) to support safer outdoor work. It addresses climate resilience for construction workers exposed to humid coastal heat in Tamil Nadu. Relevant SDGs: SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being), SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth), and SDG 13 (Climate Action).

## Problem statement
Construction workers in Tamil Nadu often follow fixed shifts during humid heat. A blanket midday break cannot account for hourly humidity, solar exposure, wind, or task intensity. Stopping work can also mean lost wages, creating pressure to continue in unsafe conditions. Supervisors need a site-specific, condition-based aid to decide when to work, slow down, or stop.

## Solution description
The prototype estimates hourly outdoor WBGT from weather data using the Liljegren model, applies configured ISO 7243 thresholds by work intensity, acclimatization, and clothing, then schedules work/rest periods. It generates Tamil/English supervisor messages and a stoppage log. Built with Python, Streamlit, and Open-Meteo data. KPIs: safe work-minutes, overload minutes, and comparison with fixed shifts.
